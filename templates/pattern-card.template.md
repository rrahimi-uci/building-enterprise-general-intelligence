### Pattern P-XX — Stable Pattern Name

**Problem.** What recurring condition or decision creates risk or complexity?

**Anti-Pattern.** Name the tempting but unsafe or ineffective approach.

**Why It Fails.** Describe the causal mechanism, affected invariant, and likely failure mode.

**Recommended Pattern.** State the bounded design response and the conditions under which it applies.

**Architecture.** Identify components, control/data flow, state ownership, trust boundaries, and enforcement points. Add a registered diagram only when it teaches something prose cannot.

**Enterprise Example.** Give a concrete, assumption-bounded scenario; label illustrative numbers.

**Detection Metrics.** Define observable signals, calculation, window, owner, threshold, and response.

**Trade-offs.** Address security, reliability, latency, cost, complexity, portability, governance, and organizational burden.

**Evidence.** Add narrowly scoped citations such as `[Rxx]`; state what they do not prove.
