### Threat T-XX — Stable Threat Name

**Asset at risk.** Data, capability, identity, budget, state, or decision authority.

**Trust boundary.** Where authority or data crosses a boundary.

**Adversary or precondition.** Actor, compromised component, unsafe input, or operational condition.

**Attack/failure path.** Ordered mechanism from entry to impact.

**Preventive controls.** Identity, policy, validation, isolation, capability reduction, provenance, or review.

**Detective controls.** Events, traces, metrics, alerts, canaries, reconciliation, or anomaly checks.

**Response and recovery.** Containment, revocation, rollback, replay, evidence preservation, and owner.

**Residual risk.** What remains, who accepts it, and when the decision expires.

**Evidence.** Scoped references `[Rxx]`; do not imply a checklist proves system safety.
